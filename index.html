Why this wiki

Learning fails when the path is invisible

Class notes get lost. Sprint goals stay only in the teacher's head. Students don't see the full journey, and the link between "today" and "the whole course" breaks.

What the teacher needs

  • A living Sprint Backlog that is easy to update every day
  • A Product Backlog that keeps the full history
  • Clear marks: ++ new topic, ---- pending to future
  • One place that links commits, masterDocs, diagrams and labs

What the student needs

  • Know exactly what we did today and what comes next
  • See the whole roadmap, not only the current day
  • Find reference articles and previous labs quickly
  • Feel progress instead of guessing
The connection

One shared path. Visible to both.

Every course edition follows the same pattern: Official docs · Sprint Backlog · Product Backlog · Calendar & Deliveries. The Sprint is the live plan for the week; when the week ends it is pasted into the Product Backlog and a new Sprint starts.

🗂

Sprint Backlog

The plan for this week, day by day, with numbered steps. ++ for new topics, ---- for what moves to the future.
🗃

Product Backlog

The permanent history. Every finished week is pasted here, month by month, so the full journey stays visible.
📅

Calendar & Deliveries

Official dates, evaluation weights (30% continuous, 70% final) and how to deliver the exercises.
📜

Official Docs

The Certificat de Professionalitat: modules, hours and the general competence of the course.
The weekly rhythm

How we keep the path alive

1

Plan the Sprint

Monday: write the sprint header and the daily topics with numbered steps.

2

Update every day

Mark progress, add ++ for new topics, strike what we skip, link the commit and the masterDoc.

3

Close the week

Paste the finished week into the Product Backlog. Start a fresh Sprint.

4

Students always see the truth

The pages the teacher updates are the pages students open. No hidden plan. Why SCRUM →

-topic planned or done
----topic pending to future
++new topic not planned
